========================================================================
TERM SHEET
FOR SERIES SEED PREFERRED STOCK FINANCING OF
QUANTUM GENOMICS, INC.

Date: 15 July 2022

Issuer:           Quantum Genomics, Inc. (the "Company")
Investors:        Coatue Management (the "Lead Investor")
Series:           Series Seed Preferred Stock
Pre-Money Valuation:  $80.0 million
Amount of Financing:  $36.3 million
========================================================================

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms of the proposed financing.
Except for the binding provisions set forth below, this term sheet is
non-binding and is intended solely as a basis for further discussion.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Liquidation Preference
The Series Seed Preferred Stock shall have a liquidation preference equal to 1.0x the Original Purchase Price per share. The Series Seed Preferred Stock shall be non-participating.Any remaining proceeds shall be distributed pro rata among holders of Common Stock.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

ANTI-DILUTION PROTECTION
In the event of a down-round issuance, the conversion price of the Series Seed Preferred shall be adjusted pursuant to a broad-based weighted average formula.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Board of Directors
The Board shall have 4 seats, allocated as follows: Investors shall designate 1 director(s), Founders shall designate 2 director(s), and 1 independent director(s) mutually agreed upon by the Common and Preferred holders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Founder Share Vesting
48 months total vesting schedule. Cliff: 12 months. Monthly vesting thereafter. Acceleration shall be double-trigger, requiring both a Change of Control event and involuntary termination.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

DIVIDEND RIGHTS
The Series Seed Preferred shall accrue non-cumulative dividends at 8% of the Original Issue Price, payable when and if declared by the Board.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Protective Provisions
So long as any shares of Series Seed Preferred Stock are outstanding, the Company shall not, without the prior written consent of the holders of at least a majority of the Series Seed Preferred Stock: (i) alter or change the rights of the Preferred Stock; (ii) increase or decrease the authorized number of shares; (iii) create any new class or series of shares having rights senior to or on parity with the Preferred Stock; (iv) declare or pay any dividend on Common Stock; (v) approve any merger, sale of substantially all assets, or other liquidation event; or (vi) increase the size of the Board of Directors.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Option Pool
Prior to the closing of the financing, the Company shall reserve 15% of its post-money fully-diluted capitalization for issuance to directors, officers, employees, and consultants under the Company's equity incentive plan.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

INFORMATION AND INSPECTION RIGHTS
Major Investors shall receive annual audited financials, quarterly unaudited financials, monthly management reports, and an annual operating budget.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Right of First Refusal / Co-Sale
The Company shall have a right of first refusal on any proposed transfer of shares by a Founder. If the Company does not exercise its right, the Investors holding Series Seed Preferred Stock shall have a secondary right of first refusal, followed by a co-sale right allowing participation in the transfer pro rata.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CONVERSION
The Series Seed Preferred converts to Common Stock at a 1:1 ratio (as adjusted). Automatic conversion occurs upon a Qualified IPO or majority Preferred consent.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

DRAG-ALONG RIGHTS
A Sale of the Company approved by 67% or more of the outstanding shares shall be binding on all shareholders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Mandatory Redemption
The Series Seed Preferred Stock is redeemable at the option of the holders commencing 10 years from the Closing Date.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

NO-SHOP AGREEMENT
For 45 days from execution of this term sheet, the Company and the Founders shall not directly or indirectly solicit, initiate, or encourage any competing financing proposal.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Governing Law
This Term Sheet shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws provisions.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Expenses
The Company shall pay the reasonable legal fees and expenses of counsel to the Investors in connection with the financing, up to a maximum of $25,000.
