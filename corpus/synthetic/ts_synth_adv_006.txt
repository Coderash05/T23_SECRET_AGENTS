========================================================================
TERM SHEET
FOR SERIES B PREFERRED STOCK FINANCING OF
CIPHER ROBOTICS, INC.

Date: 15 December 2022

Issuer:           Cipher Robotics, Inc. (the "Company")
Investors:        Founders Fund (the "Lead Investor")
Series:           Series B Preferred Stock
Pre-Money Valuation:  $20.0 million
Amount of Financing:  $6.0 million
========================================================================

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms of the proposed financing.
Except for the binding provisions set forth below, this term sheet is
non-binding and is intended solely as a basis for further discussion.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Liquidation Preference
The Series B Preferred Stock shall have a liquidation preference equal to 5.0x the Original Purchase Price per share. No participation rights beyond the initial Liquidation Preference amount.Any remaining proceeds shall be distributed pro rata among holders of Common Stock.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Price-Based Anti-Dilution
The Series B Preferred Stock will have full ratchet anti-dilution protection.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Board of Directors
The Board of Directors shall consist of 4 members: 3 seat(s) designated by the holders of Series B Preferred Stock, 1 seat(s) designated by the holders of Common Stock.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

VESTING
Founder shares will vest over a period of 5.0 years with a 12-month cliff period. After the cliff, 1.67% of the remaining shares shall vest each month. Single-trigger acceleration: all unvested shares shall immediately vest upon a Change of Control.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Dividends
Dividends shall be cumulative at a rate of 8% per annum on the Series B Preferred Stock. No dividends shall be paid on Common Stock until all Preferred dividends are satisfied.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

PROTECTIVE PROVISIONS
The consent of the majority of Series B Preferred holders shall be required for: amendments to the Charter affecting the Preferred Stock; issuance of equity securities senior to the Series B Preferred; any Liquidation Event or Change of Control; changes to the Board size; and declaration of dividends on Common Stock.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Option Pool
Prior to the closing of the financing, the Company shall reserve 25% of its post-money fully-diluted capitalization for issuance to directors, officers, employees, and consultants under the Company's equity incentive plan.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Information Rights
The Company shall deliver to each Major Investor (i) annual audited financial statements within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year, (ii) unaudited quarterly financial statements within 45 days after the end of each quarter, and (iii) an annual budget and business plan within 30 days prior to the start of each fiscal year.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Right of First Refusal / Co-Sale
The Company shall have a right of first refusal on any proposed transfer of shares by a Founder. If the Company does not exercise its right, the Investors holding Series B Preferred Stock shall have a secondary right of first refusal, followed by a co-sale right allowing participation in the transfer pro rata.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Conversion Rights
Each share of Series B Preferred Stock shall be convertible, at the option of the holder, into shares of Common Stock at the then-applicable Conversion Rate (initially 1:1, subject to anti-dilution adjustments). All Series B Preferred Stock shall automatically convert into Common Stock upon (i) an IPO with aggregate proceeds of at least $50,000,000 or (ii) the consent of a majority of the Preferred holders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Drag-Along
Shareholders holding at least 10% of the shares may compel all remaining shareholders to participate in an approved Sale of the Company.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

REDEMPTION
Beginning 2 years after closing, the holders of Series B Preferred may require the Company to redeem their shares at the Original Issue Price.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Exclusivity / No-Shop
The Company agrees that for a period of 60 days following the execution of this Term Sheet, it shall not solicit, encourage, or accept any proposal from any other party for the financing of the Company.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Expenses
The Company shall pay the reasonable legal fees and expenses of counsel to the Investors in connection with the financing, up to a maximum of $25,000.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Binding Provisions
This Term Sheet is non-binding, except for the provisions relating to Confidentiality, Exclusivity, and Expenses, which shall be binding agreements of the parties.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Confidentiality
The existence and terms of this Term Sheet shall be treated as confidential information by all parties and shall not be disclosed to any third party without the prior written consent of the other party, except as required by law.
