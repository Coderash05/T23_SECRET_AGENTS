========================================================================
TERM SHEET
FOR SERIES A-1 PREFERRED STOCK FINANCING OF
IONIC SCIENTIFIC, INC.

Date: 23 June 2023

Issuer:           Ionic Scientific, Inc. (the "Company")
Investors:        Coatue Management (the "Lead Investor")
Series:           Series A-1 Preferred Stock
Pre-Money Valuation:  $15.0 million
Amount of Financing:  $3.3 million
========================================================================

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms of the proposed financing.
Except for the binding provisions set forth below, this term sheet is
non-binding and is intended solely as a basis for further discussion.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

LIQUIDATION PREFERENCE
Upon any Liquidation Event, each holder of Series A-1 Preferred shall receive a per-share amount equal to 5.0 times the Original Issue Price, plus any declared but unpaid dividends. Full participation: following payment of the Liquidation Preference, holders of Series A-1 Preferred shall share in remaining proceeds alongside Common holders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Anti-Dilution Provisions
The Conversion Price of the Series A-1 Preferred Stock shall be subject to full ratchet anti-dilution adjustment in the event that the Company issues additional equity securities at a purchase price less than the then-effective Conversion Price.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Board of Directors
The Board shall have 5 seats, allocated as follows: Investors shall designate 3 director(s), Founders shall designate 2 director(s).

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

VESTING
Founder shares will vest over a period of 5.0 years with a 12-month cliff period. After the cliff, 1.67% of the remaining shares shall vest each month. Vesting shall not accelerate under any circumstances.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

DIVIDEND RIGHTS
The Series A-1 Preferred shall accrue cumulative dividends at 8% of the Original Issue Price, payable when and if declared by the Board.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Protective Provisions
So long as any shares of Series A-1 Preferred Stock are outstanding, the Company shall not, without the prior written consent of the holders of at least a majority of the Series A-1 Preferred Stock: (i) alter or change the rights of the Preferred Stock; (ii) increase or decrease the authorized number of shares; (iii) create any new class or series of shares having rights senior to or on parity with the Preferred Stock; (iv) declare or pay any dividend on Common Stock; (v) approve any merger, sale of substantially all assets, or other liquidation event; or (vi) increase the size of the Board of Directors.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Option Pool
Prior to the closing of the financing, the Company shall reserve 25% of its post-money fully-diluted capitalization for issuance to directors, officers, employees, and consultants under the Company's equity incentive plan.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

INFORMATION AND INSPECTION RIGHTS
Major Investors shall receive annual audited financials, quarterly unaudited financials, monthly management reports, and an annual operating budget.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Right of First Refusal / Co-Sale
The Company shall have a right of first refusal on any proposed transfer of shares by a Founder. If the Company does not exercise its right, the Investors holding Series A-1 Preferred Stock shall have a secondary right of first refusal, followed by a co-sale right allowing participation in the transfer pro rata.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Conversion Rights
Each share of Series A-1 Preferred Stock shall be convertible, at the option of the holder, into shares of Common Stock at the then-applicable Conversion Rate (initially 1:1, subject to anti-dilution adjustments). All Series A-1 Preferred Stock shall automatically convert into Common Stock upon (i) an IPO with aggregate proceeds of at least $50,000,000 or (ii) the consent of a majority of the Preferred holders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Drag-Along
Shareholders holding at least 50% of the shares may compel all remaining shareholders to participate in an approved Sale of the Company.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Redemption Rights
At any time after 3 years from the date of issuance, upon the request of the holders of at least a majority of the then-outstanding Series A-1 Preferred Stock, the Company shall redeem all outstanding shares at the Original Purchase Price plus any declared but unpaid dividends.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Exclusivity Period
The Founders agree to an exclusivity period of 60 days during which no competing offers shall be sought or entertained.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Expenses
The Company shall pay the reasonable legal fees and expenses of counsel to the Investors in connection with the financing, up to a maximum of $25,000.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Binding Provisions
This Term Sheet is non-binding, except for the provisions relating to Confidentiality, Exclusivity, and Expenses, which shall be binding agreements of the parties.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Governing Law
This Term Sheet shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws provisions.
