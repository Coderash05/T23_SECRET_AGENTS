========================================================================
TERM SHEET
FOR SERIES B PREFERRED STOCK FINANCING OF
AETHER DYNAMICS, INC.

Date: 1 July 2024

Issuer:           Aether Dynamics, Inc. (the "Company")
Investors:        Bessemer Venture Partners (the "Lead Investor")
Series:           Series B Preferred Stock
Pre-Money Valuation:  $5.0 million
Amount of Financing:  $1.0 million
========================================================================

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms of the proposed financing.
Except for the binding provisions set forth below, this term sheet is
non-binding and is intended solely as a basis for further discussion.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

LIQUIDATION PREFERENCE
Upon any Liquidation Event, each holder of Series B Preferred shall receive a per-share amount equal to 2.0 times the Original Issue Price, plus any declared but unpaid dividends. Holders of Series B Preferred shall not participate in remaining distributions beyond the Liquidation Preference.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

ANTI-DILUTION PROTECTION
In the event of a down-round issuance, the conversion price of the Series B Preferred shall be adjusted pursuant to a full ratchet formula.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Board of Directors
The Board shall have 5 seats, allocated as follows: Investors shall designate 4 director(s), Founders shall designate 1 director(s).

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Founder Vesting
All shares held by each Founder shall be subject to vesting over 4.0 years (48 months total), with a 24-month cliff. Shares shall vest monthly following the cliff on a pro-rata basis. Vesting shall not accelerate under any circumstances.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Dividends
The holders of Series B Preferred Stock shall be entitled to receive non-cumulative dividends at a rate of 8% per annum on the Original Purchase Price. Dividends on Common Stock may not be paid unless all accrued Preferred dividends have been paid.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

PROTECTIVE PROVISIONS
The consent of the majority of Series B Preferred holders shall be required for: amendments to the Charter affecting the Preferred Stock; issuance of equity securities senior to the Series B Preferred; any Liquidation Event or Change of Control; changes to the Board size; and declaration of dividends on Common Stock.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Option Pool
Prior to the closing of the financing, the Company shall reserve 20% of its post-money fully-diluted capitalization for issuance to directors, officers, employees, and consultants under the Company's equity incentive plan.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Information Rights
The Company shall deliver to each Major Investor (i) annual audited financial statements within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year, (ii) unaudited quarterly financial statements within 45 days after the end of each quarter, and (iii) an annual budget and business plan within 30 days prior to the start of each fiscal year.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Right of First Refusal / Co-Sale
The Company shall have a right of first refusal on any proposed transfer of shares by a Founder. If the Company does not exercise its right, the Investors holding Series B Preferred Stock shall have a secondary right of first refusal, followed by a co-sale right allowing participation in the transfer pro rata.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CONVERSION
The Series B Preferred converts to Common Stock at a 1:1 ratio (as adjusted). Automatic conversion occurs upon a Qualified IPO or majority Preferred consent.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Drag-Along Agreement
If holders of at least 50% of the outstanding shares approve a Sale of the Company, all other shareholders shall be required to vote in favor of such transaction and take all necessary actions to consummate it.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Redemption Rights
At any time after 1 years from the date of issuance, upon the request of the holders of at least a majority of the then-outstanding Series B Preferred Stock, the Company shall redeem all outstanding shares at the Original Purchase Price plus any declared but unpaid dividends.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Exclusivity Period
The Founders agree to an exclusivity period of 180 days during which no competing offers shall be sought or entertained.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Confidentiality
The existence and terms of this Term Sheet shall be treated as confidential information by all parties and shall not be disclosed to any third party without the prior written consent of the other party, except as required by law.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Expenses
The Company shall pay the reasonable legal fees and expenses of counsel to the Investors in connection with the financing, up to a maximum of $25,000.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Governing Law
This Term Sheet shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws provisions.
