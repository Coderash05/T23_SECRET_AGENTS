========================================================================
TERM SHEET
FOR SERIES A PREFERRED STOCK FINANCING OF
NIMBUS GENOMICS, INC.

Date: 5 September 2026

Issuer:           Nimbus Genomics, Inc. (the "Company")
Investors:        First Round Capital (the "Lead Investor")
Series:           Series A Preferred Stock
Pre-Money Valuation:  $30.0 million
Amount of Financing:  $14.6 million
========================================================================

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms of the proposed financing.
Except for the binding provisions set forth below, this term sheet is
non-binding and is intended solely as a basis for further discussion.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Liquidation Preference
In the event of any liquidation, dissolution, or winding up of the Company, the holders of Series Series A Preferred Stock shall be entitled to receive, prior and in preference to any distribution to the holders of Common Stock, an amount per share equal to 1.0x the Original Purchase Price (the "Liquidation Preference"). Holders of Series A Preferred shall not participate in remaining distributions beyond the Liquidation Preference.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

ANTI-DILUTION PROTECTION
In the event of a down-round issuance, the conversion price of the Series A Preferred shall be adjusted pursuant to a broad-based weighted average formula.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Board of Directors
The Board of Directors shall consist of 5 members: 2 seat(s) designated by the holders of Series A Preferred Stock, 2 seat(s) designated by the holders of Common Stock, and 1 independent director(s) mutually agreed upon by the Common and Preferred holders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Founder Vesting
All shares held by each Founder shall be subject to vesting over 4.0 years (48 months total), with a 12-month cliff. Shares shall vest monthly following the cliff on a pro-rata basis. Vesting shall not accelerate under any circumstances.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Dividends
Dividends shall be cumulative at a rate of 8% per annum on the Series A Preferred Stock. No dividends shall be paid on Common Stock until all Preferred dividends are satisfied.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Protective Provisions
So long as any shares of Series A Preferred Stock are outstanding, the Company shall not, without the prior written consent of the holders of at least a majority of the Series A Preferred Stock: (i) alter or change the rights of the Preferred Stock; (ii) increase or decrease the authorized number of shares; (iii) create any new class or series of shares having rights senior to or on parity with the Preferred Stock; (iv) declare or pay any dividend on Common Stock; (v) approve any merger, sale of substantially all assets, or other liquidation event; or (vi) increase the size of the Board of Directors.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

EMPLOYEE OPTION POOL
An option pool equal to 10% of the post-money fully-diluted shares shall be established prior to closing.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Information Rights
The Company shall deliver to each Major Investor (i) annual audited financial statements within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year, (ii) unaudited quarterly financial statements within 45 days after the end of each quarter, and (iii) an annual budget and business plan within 30 days prior to the start of each fiscal year.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Right of First Refusal / Co-Sale
The Company shall have a right of first refusal on any proposed transfer of shares by a Founder. If the Company does not exercise its right, the Investors holding Series A Preferred Stock shall have a secondary right of first refusal, followed by a co-sale right allowing participation in the transfer pro rata.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CONVERSION
The Series A Preferred converts to Common Stock at a 1:1 ratio (as adjusted). Automatic conversion occurs upon a Qualified IPO or majority Preferred consent.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

DRAG-ALONG RIGHTS
A Sale of the Company approved by 67% or more of the outstanding shares shall be binding on all shareholders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

REDEMPTION
Beginning 5 years after closing, the holders of Series A Preferred may require the Company to redeem their shares at the Original Issue Price.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Exclusivity Period
The Founders agree to an exclusivity period of 45 days during which no competing offers shall be sought or entertained.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Conditions Precedent
The closing of the financing shall be subject to customary conditions, including: completion of due diligence satisfactory to the Investors; execution of definitive agreements; delivery of legal opinions; and all requisite corporate approvals.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Governing Law
This Term Sheet shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws provisions.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Expenses
The Company shall pay the reasonable legal fees and expenses of counsel to the Investors in connection with the financing, up to a maximum of $25,000.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Binding Provisions
This Term Sheet is non-binding, except for the provisions relating to Confidentiality, Exclusivity, and Expenses, which shall be binding agreements of the parties.
