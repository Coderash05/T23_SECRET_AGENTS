========================================================================
TERM SHEET
FOR SERIES C PREFERRED STOCK FINANCING OF
NOVA VENTURES, INC.

Date: 19 May 2022

Issuer:           Nova Ventures, Inc. (the "Company")
Investors:        Bessemer Venture Partners (the "Lead Investor")
Series:           Series C Preferred Stock
Pre-Money Valuation:  $25.0 million
Amount of Financing:  $5.7 million
========================================================================

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms of the proposed financing.
Except for the binding provisions set forth below, this term sheet is
non-binding and is intended solely as a basis for further discussion.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Liquidation Preference
The Series C Preferred Stock shall have a liquidation preference equal to 3.0x the Original Purchase Price per share. After receiving the Liquidation Preference, the Series C Preferred shall participate pro rata with the Common Stock on an as-converted basis, with no cap.Any remaining proceeds shall be distributed pro rata among holders of Common Stock.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

ANTI-DILUTION PROTECTION
In the event of a down-round issuance, the conversion price of the Series C Preferred shall be adjusted pursuant to a full ratchet formula.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

BOARD COMPOSITION
The Company's Board shall be composed of 4 directors: 3 appointed by the Investors, 1 appointed by the Founders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Founder Vesting
All shares held by each Founder shall be subject to vesting over 5.0 years (60 months total), with a 12-month cliff. Shares shall vest monthly following the cliff on a pro-rata basis. Vesting shall not accelerate under any circumstances.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Dividends
The holders of Series C Preferred Stock shall be entitled to receive cumulative dividends at a rate of 8% per annum on the Original Purchase Price. Dividends on Common Stock may not be paid unless all accrued Preferred dividends have been paid.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

PROTECTIVE PROVISIONS
The consent of the majority of Series C Preferred holders shall be required for: amendments to the Charter affecting the Preferred Stock; issuance of equity securities senior to the Series C Preferred; any Liquidation Event or Change of Control; changes to the Board size; and declaration of dividends on Common Stock.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Option Pool
Prior to the closing of the financing, the Company shall reserve 30% of its post-money fully-diluted capitalization for issuance to directors, officers, employees, and consultants under the Company's equity incentive plan.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Information Rights
The Company shall deliver to each Major Investor (i) annual audited financial statements within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year, (ii) unaudited quarterly financial statements within 45 days after the end of each quarter, and (iii) an annual budget and business plan within 30 days prior to the start of each fiscal year.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

ROFR AND CO-SALE AGREEMENT
Founders may not transfer shares without first offering them to the Company, then to the Investors. If the transfer proceeds, Investors have co-sale rights to participate on a pro-rata basis.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Conversion Rights
Each share of Series C Preferred Stock shall be convertible, at the option of the holder, into shares of Common Stock at the then-applicable Conversion Rate (initially 1:1, subject to anti-dilution adjustments). All Series C Preferred Stock shall automatically convert into Common Stock upon (i) an IPO with aggregate proceeds of at least $50,000,000 or (ii) the consent of a majority of the Preferred holders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

DRAG-ALONG RIGHTS
A Sale of the Company approved by 25% or more of the outstanding shares shall be binding on all shareholders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

REDEMPTION
Beginning 2 years after closing, the holders of Series C Preferred may require the Company to redeem their shares at the Original Issue Price.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Exclusivity Period
The Founders agree to an exclusivity period of 120 days during which no competing offers shall be sought or entertained.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Governing Law
This Term Sheet shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws provisions.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Conditions Precedent
The closing of the financing shall be subject to customary conditions, including: completion of due diligence satisfactory to the Investors; execution of definitive agreements; delivery of legal opinions; and all requisite corporate approvals.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Binding Provisions
This Term Sheet is non-binding, except for the provisions relating to Confidentiality, Exclusivity, and Expenses, which shall be binding agreements of the parties.
