========================================================================
TERM SHEET
FOR SERIES A-1 PREFERRED STOCK FINANCING OF
ZENITH ANALYTICS, INC.

Date: 28 August 2022

Issuer:           Zenith Analytics, Inc. (the "Company")
Investors:        Tiger Global Management (the "Lead Investor")
Series:           Series A-1 Preferred Stock
Pre-Money Valuation:  $12.0 million
Amount of Financing:  $6.0 million
========================================================================

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms of the proposed financing.
Except for the binding provisions set forth below, this term sheet is
non-binding and is intended solely as a basis for further discussion.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

LIQUIDATION PREFERENCE
Upon any Liquidation Event, each holder of Series A-1 Preferred shall receive a per-share amount equal to 1.0 times the Original Issue Price, plus any declared but unpaid dividends. After receiving the Liquidation Preference, the Series A-1 Preferred shall participate pro rata with the Common Stock on an as-converted basis, with no cap.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Price-Based Anti-Dilution
The Series A-1 Preferred Stock will have broad-based weighted average anti-dilution protection.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

BOARD COMPOSITION
The Company's Board shall be composed of 5 directors: 2 appointed by the Investors, 2 appointed by the Founders, and 1 independent director(s) mutually agreed upon by the Common and Preferred holders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Founder Share Vesting
48 months total vesting schedule. Cliff: 6 months. Monthly vesting thereafter. Acceleration shall be double-trigger, requiring both a Change of Control event and involuntary termination.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

DIVIDEND RIGHTS
The Series A-1 Preferred shall accrue cumulative dividends at 8% of the Original Issue Price, payable when and if declared by the Board.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Protective Provisions
So long as any shares of Series A-1 Preferred Stock are outstanding, the Company shall not, without the prior written consent of the holders of at least a majority of the Series A-1 Preferred Stock: (i) alter or change the rights of the Preferred Stock; (ii) increase or decrease the authorized number of shares; (iii) create any new class or series of shares having rights senior to or on parity with the Preferred Stock; (iv) declare or pay any dividend on Common Stock; (v) approve any merger, sale of substantially all assets, or other liquidation event; or (vi) increase the size of the Board of Directors.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Equity Incentive Pool
The Company's Option Pool shall be set at 15% of the fully-diluted post-money capitalization, to be created prior to the Closing.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

INFORMATION AND INSPECTION RIGHTS
Major Investors shall receive annual audited financials, quarterly unaudited financials, monthly management reports, and an annual operating budget.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

ROFR AND CO-SALE AGREEMENT
Founders may not transfer shares without first offering them to the Company, then to the Investors. If the transfer proceeds, Investors have co-sale rights to participate on a pro-rata basis.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Conversion Rights
Each share of Series A-1 Preferred Stock shall be convertible, at the option of the holder, into shares of Common Stock at the then-applicable Conversion Rate (initially 1:1, subject to anti-dilution adjustments). All Series A-1 Preferred Stock shall automatically convert into Common Stock upon (i) an IPO with aggregate proceeds of at least $50,000,000 or (ii) the consent of a majority of the Preferred holders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Drag-Along
Shareholders holding at least 67% of the shares may compel all remaining shareholders to participate in an approved Sale of the Company.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Mandatory Redemption
The Series A-1 Preferred Stock is redeemable at the option of the holders commencing 5 years from the Closing Date.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Exclusivity Period
The Founders agree to an exclusivity period of 30 days during which no competing offers shall be sought or entertained.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Confidentiality
The existence and terms of this Term Sheet shall be treated as confidential information by all parties and shall not be disclosed to any third party without the prior written consent of the other party, except as required by law.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Conditions Precedent
The closing of the financing shall be subject to customary conditions, including: completion of due diligence satisfactory to the Investors; execution of definitive agreements; delivery of legal opinions; and all requisite corporate approvals.
