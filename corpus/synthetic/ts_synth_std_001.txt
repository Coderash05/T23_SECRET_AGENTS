========================================================================
TERM SHEET
FOR SERIES B PREFERRED STOCK FINANCING OF
NEXUS GENOMICS, INC.

Date: 16 January 2026

Issuer:           Nexus Genomics, Inc. (the "Company")
Investors:        Index Ventures (the "Lead Investor")
Series:           Series B Preferred Stock
Pre-Money Valuation:  $15.0 million
Amount of Financing:  $6.6 million
========================================================================

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms of the proposed financing.
Except for the binding provisions set forth below, this term sheet is
non-binding and is intended solely as a basis for further discussion.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

LIQUIDATION PREFERENCE
Upon any Liquidation Event, each holder of Series B Preferred shall receive a per-share amount equal to 3.0 times the Original Issue Price, plus any declared but unpaid dividends. The Series B Preferred Stock shall be non-participating.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Anti-Dilution Provisions
The Conversion Price of the Series B Preferred Stock shall be subject to full ratchet anti-dilution adjustment in the event that the Company issues additional equity securities at a purchase price less than the then-effective Conversion Price.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Board of Directors
The Board shall have 5 seats, allocated as follows: Investors shall designate 2 director(s), Founders shall designate 2 director(s), and 1 independent director(s) mutually agreed upon by the Common and Preferred holders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Founder Share Vesting
48 months total vesting schedule. Cliff: 12 months. Monthly vesting thereafter. Double-trigger acceleration: 100% of unvested shares shall accelerate upon a Change of Control followed by termination without Cause within 12 months.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Dividends
The holders of Series B Preferred Stock shall be entitled to receive non-cumulative dividends at a rate of 8% per annum on the Original Purchase Price. Dividends on Common Stock may not be paid unless all accrued Preferred dividends have been paid.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Protective Provisions
So long as any shares of Series B Preferred Stock are outstanding, the Company shall not, without the prior written consent of the holders of at least a majority of the Series B Preferred Stock: (i) alter or change the rights of the Preferred Stock; (ii) increase or decrease the authorized number of shares; (iii) create any new class or series of shares having rights senior to or on parity with the Preferred Stock; (iv) declare or pay any dividend on Common Stock; (v) approve any merger, sale of substantially all assets, or other liquidation event; or (vi) increase the size of the Board of Directors.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Option Pool
Prior to the closing of the financing, the Company shall reserve 10% of its post-money fully-diluted capitalization for issuance to directors, officers, employees, and consultants under the Company's equity incentive plan.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Information Rights
The Company shall deliver to each Major Investor (i) annual audited financial statements within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year, (ii) unaudited quarterly financial statements within 45 days after the end of each quarter, and (iii) an annual budget and business plan within 30 days prior to the start of each fiscal year.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Right of First Refusal / Co-Sale
The Company shall have a right of first refusal on any proposed transfer of shares by a Founder. If the Company does not exercise its right, the Investors holding Series B Preferred Stock shall have a secondary right of first refusal, followed by a co-sale right allowing participation in the transfer pro rata.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Conversion Rights
Each share of Series B Preferred Stock shall be convertible, at the option of the holder, into shares of Common Stock at the then-applicable Conversion Rate (initially 1:1, subject to anti-dilution adjustments). All Series B Preferred Stock shall automatically convert into Common Stock upon (i) an IPO with aggregate proceeds of at least $50,000,000 or (ii) the consent of a majority of the Preferred holders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Drag-Along Agreement
If holders of at least 75% of the outstanding shares approve a Sale of the Company, all other shareholders shall be required to vote in favor of such transaction and take all necessary actions to consummate it.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Redemption Rights
At any time after 5 years from the date of issuance, upon the request of the holders of at least a majority of the then-outstanding Series B Preferred Stock, the Company shall redeem all outstanding shares at the Original Purchase Price plus any declared but unpaid dividends.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

NO-SHOP AGREEMENT
For 45 days from execution of this term sheet, the Company and the Founders shall not directly or indirectly solicit, initiate, or encourage any competing financing proposal.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Governing Law
This Term Sheet shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws provisions.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Expenses
The Company shall pay the reasonable legal fees and expenses of counsel to the Investors in connection with the financing, up to a maximum of $25,000.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Conditions Precedent
The closing of the financing shall be subject to customary conditions, including: completion of due diligence satisfactory to the Investors; execution of definitive agreements; delivery of legal opinions; and all requisite corporate approvals.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Binding Provisions
This Term Sheet is non-binding, except for the provisions relating to Confidentiality, Exclusivity, and Expenses, which shall be binding agreements of the parties.
