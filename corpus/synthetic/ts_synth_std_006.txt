========================================================================
TERM SHEET
FOR SERIES SEED PREFERRED STOCK FINANCING OF
PRISM HEALTH, INC.

Date: 15 December 2026

Issuer:           Prism Health, Inc. (the "Company")
Investors:        Thrive Capital (the "Lead Investor")
Series:           Series Seed Preferred Stock
Pre-Money Valuation:  $10.0 million
Amount of Financing:  $1.5 million
========================================================================

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms of the proposed financing.
Except for the binding provisions set forth below, this term sheet is
non-binding and is intended solely as a basis for further discussion.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

LIQUIDATION PREFERENCE
Upon any Liquidation Event, each holder of Series Seed Preferred shall receive a per-share amount equal to 1.0 times the Original Issue Price, plus any declared but unpaid dividends. Participation is capped at an aggregate of 2.0x the Original Purchase Price per share.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Anti-Dilution Provisions
The Conversion Price of the Series Seed Preferred Stock shall be subject to broad-based weighted average anti-dilution adjustment in the event that the Company issues additional equity securities at a purchase price less than the then-effective Conversion Price.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Board of Directors
The Board of Directors shall consist of 5 members: 2 seat(s) designated by the holders of Series Seed Preferred Stock, 2 seat(s) designated by the holders of Common Stock, and 1 independent director(s) mutually agreed upon by the Common and Preferred holders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Founder Share Vesting
48 months total vesting schedule. Cliff: 12 months. Monthly vesting thereafter. Acceleration shall be double-trigger, requiring both a Change of Control event and involuntary termination.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

DIVIDEND RIGHTS
The Series Seed Preferred shall accrue cumulative dividends at 8% of the Original Issue Price, payable when and if declared by the Board.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

PROTECTIVE PROVISIONS
The consent of the majority of Series Seed Preferred holders shall be required for: amendments to the Charter affecting the Preferred Stock; issuance of equity securities senior to the Series Seed Preferred; any Liquidation Event or Change of Control; changes to the Board size; and declaration of dividends on Common Stock.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Option Pool
Prior to the closing of the financing, the Company shall reserve 15% of its post-money fully-diluted capitalization for issuance to directors, officers, employees, and consultants under the Company's equity incentive plan.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Information Rights
The Company shall deliver to each Major Investor (i) annual audited financial statements within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year, (ii) unaudited quarterly financial statements within 45 days after the end of each quarter, and (iii) an annual budget and business plan within 30 days prior to the start of each fiscal year.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

ROFR AND CO-SALE AGREEMENT
Founders may not transfer shares without first offering them to the Company, then to the Investors. If the transfer proceeds, Investors have co-sale rights to participate on a pro-rata basis.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Conversion Rights
Each share of Series Seed Preferred Stock shall be convertible, at the option of the holder, into shares of Common Stock at the then-applicable Conversion Rate (initially 1:1, subject to anti-dilution adjustments). All Series Seed Preferred Stock shall automatically convert into Common Stock upon (i) an IPO with aggregate proceeds of at least $50,000,000 or (ii) the consent of a majority of the Preferred holders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

DRAG-ALONG RIGHTS
A Sale of the Company approved by 90% or more of the outstanding shares shall be binding on all shareholders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Redemption Rights
At any time after 5 years from the date of issuance, upon the request of the holders of at least a majority of the then-outstanding Series Seed Preferred Stock, the Company shall redeem all outstanding shares at the Original Purchase Price plus any declared but unpaid dividends.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Exclusivity Period
The Founders agree to an exclusivity period of 30 days during which no competing offers shall be sought or entertained.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Expenses
The Company shall pay the reasonable legal fees and expenses of counsel to the Investors in connection with the financing, up to a maximum of $25,000.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Binding Provisions
This Term Sheet is non-binding, except for the provisions relating to Confidentiality, Exclusivity, and Expenses, which shall be binding agreements of the parties.
