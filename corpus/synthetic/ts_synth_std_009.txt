========================================================================
TERM SHEET
FOR SERIES B PREFERRED STOCK FINANCING OF
NIMBUS ANALYTICS, INC.

Date: 18 March 2025

Issuer:           Nimbus Analytics, Inc. (the "Company")
Investors:        Insight Partners (the "Lead Investor")
Series:           Series B Preferred Stock
Pre-Money Valuation:  $30.0 million
Amount of Financing:  $7.1 million
========================================================================

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms of the proposed financing.
Except for the binding provisions set forth below, this term sheet is
non-binding and is intended solely as a basis for further discussion.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Liquidation Preference
The Series B Preferred Stock shall have a liquidation preference equal to 1.5x the Original Purchase Price per share. The Series B Preferred Stock shall be non-participating.Any remaining proceeds shall be distributed pro rata among holders of Common Stock.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Price-Based Anti-Dilution
The Series B Preferred Stock will have broad-based weighted average anti-dilution protection.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Board of Directors
The Board shall have 5 seats, allocated as follows: Investors shall designate 2 director(s), Founders shall designate 2 director(s), and 1 independent director(s) mutually agreed upon by the Common and Preferred holders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Founder Vesting
All shares held by each Founder shall be subject to vesting over 4.0 years (48 months total), with a 12-month cliff. Shares shall vest monthly following the cliff on a pro-rata basis. Double-trigger acceleration: 100% of unvested shares shall accelerate upon a Change of Control followed by termination without Cause within 12 months.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

DIVIDEND RIGHTS
The Series B Preferred shall accrue non-cumulative dividends at 8% of the Original Issue Price, payable when and if declared by the Board.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

PROTECTIVE PROVISIONS
The consent of the majority of Series B Preferred holders shall be required for: amendments to the Charter affecting the Preferred Stock; issuance of equity securities senior to the Series B Preferred; any Liquidation Event or Change of Control; changes to the Board size; and declaration of dividends on Common Stock.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

EMPLOYEE OPTION POOL
An option pool equal to 20% of the post-money fully-diluted shares shall be established prior to closing.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

INFORMATION AND INSPECTION RIGHTS
Major Investors shall receive annual audited financials, quarterly unaudited financials, monthly management reports, and an annual operating budget.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Right of First Refusal / Co-Sale
The Company shall have a right of first refusal on any proposed transfer of shares by a Founder. If the Company does not exercise its right, the Investors holding Series B Preferred Stock shall have a secondary right of first refusal, followed by a co-sale right allowing participation in the transfer pro rata.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CONVERSION
The Series B Preferred converts to Common Stock at a 1:1 ratio (as adjusted). Automatic conversion occurs upon a Qualified IPO or majority Preferred consent.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Drag-Along
Shareholders holding at least 90% of the shares may compel all remaining shareholders to participate in an approved Sale of the Company.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

REDEMPTION
Beginning 7 years after closing, the holders of Series B Preferred may require the Company to redeem their shares at the Original Issue Price.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Exclusivity Period
The Founders agree to an exclusivity period of 30 days during which no competing offers shall be sought or entertained.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Conditions Precedent
The closing of the financing shall be subject to customary conditions, including: completion of due diligence satisfactory to the Investors; execution of definitive agreements; delivery of legal opinions; and all requisite corporate approvals.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Expenses
The Company shall pay the reasonable legal fees and expenses of counsel to the Investors in connection with the financing, up to a maximum of $25,000.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Confidentiality
The existence and terms of this Term Sheet shall be treated as confidential information by all parties and shall not be disclosed to any third party without the prior written consent of the other party, except as required by law.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Binding Provisions
This Term Sheet is non-binding, except for the provisions relating to Confidentiality, Exclusivity, and Expenses, which shall be binding agreements of the parties.
