========================================================================
TERM SHEET
FOR SERIES B PREFERRED STOCK FINANCING OF
NOVA SCIENTIFIC, INC.

Date: 10 July 2025

Issuer:           Nova Scientific, Inc. (the "Company")
Investors:        First Round Capital (the "Lead Investor")
Series:           Series B Preferred Stock
Pre-Money Valuation:  $5.0 million
Amount of Financing:  $2.4 million
========================================================================

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms of the proposed financing.
Except for the binding provisions set forth below, this term sheet is
non-binding and is intended solely as a basis for further discussion.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Liquidation Preference
The Series B Preferred Stock shall have a liquidation preference equal to 2.0x the Original Purchase Price per share. The Series B Preferred shall be fully participating preferred stock.Any remaining proceeds shall be distributed pro rata among holders of Common Stock.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Price-Based Anti-Dilution
The Series B Preferred Stock will have full ratchet anti-dilution protection.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Board of Directors
The Board shall have 4 seats, allocated as follows: Investors shall designate 3 director(s), Founders shall designate 1 director(s).

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

VESTING
Founder shares will vest over a period of 4.0 years with a 24-month cliff period. After the cliff, 2.08% of the remaining shares shall vest each month. Double-trigger acceleration: 100% of unvested shares shall accelerate upon a Change of Control followed by termination without Cause within 12 months.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Dividends
The holders of Series B Preferred Stock shall be entitled to receive cumulative dividends at a rate of 8% per annum on the Original Purchase Price. Dividends on Common Stock may not be paid unless all accrued Preferred dividends have been paid.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

PROTECTIVE PROVISIONS
The consent of the majority of Series B Preferred holders shall be required for: amendments to the Charter affecting the Preferred Stock; issuance of equity securities senior to the Series B Preferred; any Liquidation Event or Change of Control; changes to the Board size; and declaration of dividends on Common Stock.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

EMPLOYEE OPTION POOL
An option pool equal to 25% of the post-money fully-diluted shares shall be established prior to closing.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

INFORMATION AND INSPECTION RIGHTS
Major Investors shall receive annual audited financials, quarterly unaudited financials, monthly management reports, and an annual operating budget.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Right of First Refusal / Co-Sale
The Company shall have a right of first refusal on any proposed transfer of shares by a Founder. If the Company does not exercise its right, the Investors holding Series B Preferred Stock shall have a secondary right of first refusal, followed by a co-sale right allowing participation in the transfer pro rata.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CONVERSION
The Series B Preferred converts to Common Stock at a 1:1 ratio (as adjusted). Automatic conversion occurs upon a Qualified IPO or majority Preferred consent.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

DRAG-ALONG RIGHTS
A Sale of the Company approved by 25% or more of the outstanding shares shall be binding on all shareholders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Mandatory Redemption
The Series B Preferred Stock is redeemable at the option of the holders commencing 4 years from the Closing Date.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Exclusivity Period
The Founders agree to an exclusivity period of 90 days during which no competing offers shall be sought or entertained.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Expenses
The Company shall pay the reasonable legal fees and expenses of counsel to the Investors in connection with the financing, up to a maximum of $25,000.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Conditions Precedent
The closing of the financing shall be subject to customary conditions, including: completion of due diligence satisfactory to the Investors; execution of definitive agreements; delivery of legal opinions; and all requisite corporate approvals.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Governing Law
This Term Sheet shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws provisions.
