========================================================================
TERM SHEET
FOR SERIES B PREFERRED STOCK FINANCING OF
FORGE DIGITAL, INC.

Date: 6 December 2025

Issuer:           Forge Digital, Inc. (the "Company")
Investors:        Coatue Management (the "Lead Investor")
Series:           Series B Preferred Stock
Pre-Money Valuation:  $40.0 million
Amount of Financing:  $12.2 million
========================================================================

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms of the proposed financing.
Except for the binding provisions set forth below, this term sheet is
non-binding and is intended solely as a basis for further discussion.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

LIQUIDATION PREFERENCE
Upon any Liquidation Event, each holder of Series B Preferred shall receive a per-share amount equal to 2.0 times the Original Issue Price, plus any declared but unpaid dividends. Holders of Series B Preferred shall not participate in remaining distributions beyond the Liquidation Preference.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Anti-Dilution Provisions
The Conversion Price of the Series B Preferred Stock shall be subject to full ratchet anti-dilution adjustment in the event that the Company issues additional equity securities at a purchase price less than the then-effective Conversion Price.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Board of Directors
The Board shall have 4 seats, allocated as follows: Investors shall designate 3 director(s), Founders shall designate 1 director(s).

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Founder Share Vesting
48 months total vesting schedule. Cliff: 18 months. Monthly vesting thereafter. Acceleration shall be double-trigger, requiring both a Change of Control event and involuntary termination.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

DIVIDEND RIGHTS
The Series B Preferred shall accrue non-cumulative dividends at 8% of the Original Issue Price, payable when and if declared by the Board.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Protective Provisions
So long as any shares of Series B Preferred Stock are outstanding, the Company shall not, without the prior written consent of the holders of at least a majority of the Series B Preferred Stock: (i) alter or change the rights of the Preferred Stock; (ii) increase or decrease the authorized number of shares; (iii) create any new class or series of shares having rights senior to or on parity with the Preferred Stock; (iv) declare or pay any dividend on Common Stock; (v) approve any merger, sale of substantially all assets, or other liquidation event; or (vi) increase the size of the Board of Directors.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

EMPLOYEE OPTION POOL
An option pool equal to 35% of the post-money fully-diluted shares shall be established prior to closing.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

INFORMATION AND INSPECTION RIGHTS
Major Investors shall receive annual audited financials, quarterly unaudited financials, monthly management reports, and an annual operating budget.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Right of First Refusal / Co-Sale
The Company shall have a right of first refusal on any proposed transfer of shares by a Founder. If the Company does not exercise its right, the Investors holding Series B Preferred Stock shall have a secondary right of first refusal, followed by a co-sale right allowing participation in the transfer pro rata.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CONVERSION
The Series B Preferred converts to Common Stock at a 1:1 ratio (as adjusted). Automatic conversion occurs upon a Qualified IPO or majority Preferred consent.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Drag-Along Agreement
If holders of at least 50% of the outstanding shares approve a Sale of the Company, all other shareholders shall be required to vote in favor of such transaction and take all necessary actions to consummate it.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

REDEMPTION
Beginning 2 years after closing, the holders of Series B Preferred may require the Company to redeem their shares at the Original Issue Price.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

NO-SHOP AGREEMENT
For 90 days from execution of this term sheet, the Company and the Founders shall not directly or indirectly solicit, initiate, or encourage any competing financing proposal.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Confidentiality
The existence and terms of this Term Sheet shall be treated as confidential information by all parties and shall not be disclosed to any third party without the prior written consent of the other party, except as required by law.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Governing Law
This Term Sheet shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws provisions.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Conditions Precedent
The closing of the financing shall be subject to customary conditions, including: completion of due diligence satisfactory to the Investors; execution of definitive agreements; delivery of legal opinions; and all requisite corporate approvals.
