========================================================================
TERM SHEET
FOR SERIES C PREFERRED STOCK FINANCING OF
PULSAR SYSTEMS, INC.

Date: 8 February 2024

Issuer:           Pulsar Systems, Inc. (the "Company")
Investors:        Accel Partners (the "Lead Investor")
Series:           Series C Preferred Stock
Pre-Money Valuation:  $100.0 million
Amount of Financing:  $44.1 million
========================================================================

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms of the proposed financing.
Except for the binding provisions set forth below, this term sheet is
non-binding and is intended solely as a basis for further discussion.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Liquidation Preference
The Series C Preferred Stock shall have a liquidation preference equal to 1.5x the Original Purchase Price per share. No participation rights beyond the initial Liquidation Preference amount.Any remaining proceeds shall be distributed pro rata among holders of Common Stock.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Anti-Dilution Provisions
The Conversion Price of the Series C Preferred Stock shall be subject to broad-based weighted average anti-dilution adjustment in the event that the Company issues additional equity securities at a purchase price less than the then-effective Conversion Price.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Board of Directors
The Board shall have 5 seats, allocated as follows: Investors shall designate 2 director(s), Founders shall designate 2 director(s), and 1 independent director(s) mutually agreed upon by the Common and Preferred holders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

VESTING
Founder shares will vest over a period of 4.0 years with a 12-month cliff period. After the cliff, 2.08% of the remaining shares shall vest each month. No acceleration of vesting upon Change of Control.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Dividends
Dividends shall be non-cumulative at a rate of 8% per annum on the Series C Preferred Stock. No dividends shall be paid on Common Stock until all Preferred dividends are satisfied.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

PROTECTIVE PROVISIONS
The consent of the majority of Series C Preferred holders shall be required for: amendments to the Charter affecting the Preferred Stock; issuance of equity securities senior to the Series C Preferred; any Liquidation Event or Change of Control; changes to the Board size; and declaration of dividends on Common Stock.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Equity Incentive Pool
The Company's Option Pool shall be set at 20% of the fully-diluted post-money capitalization, to be created prior to the Closing.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

INFORMATION AND INSPECTION RIGHTS
Major Investors shall receive annual audited financials, quarterly unaudited financials, monthly management reports, and an annual operating budget.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Right of First Refusal / Co-Sale
The Company shall have a right of first refusal on any proposed transfer of shares by a Founder. If the Company does not exercise its right, the Investors holding Series C Preferred Stock shall have a secondary right of first refusal, followed by a co-sale right allowing participation in the transfer pro rata.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CONVERSION
The Series C Preferred converts to Common Stock at a 1:1 ratio (as adjusted). Automatic conversion occurs upon a Qualified IPO or majority Preferred consent.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Drag-Along Agreement
If holders of at least 50% of the outstanding shares approve a Sale of the Company, all other shareholders shall be required to vote in favor of such transaction and take all necessary actions to consummate it.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

REDEMPTION
Beginning 5 years after closing, the holders of Series C Preferred may require the Company to redeem their shares at the Original Issue Price.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

NO-SHOP AGREEMENT
For 90 days from execution of this term sheet, the Company and the Founders shall not directly or indirectly solicit, initiate, or encourage any competing financing proposal.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Conditions Precedent
The closing of the financing shall be subject to customary conditions, including: completion of due diligence satisfactory to the Investors; execution of definitive agreements; delivery of legal opinions; and all requisite corporate approvals.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Binding Provisions
This Term Sheet is non-binding, except for the provisions relating to Confidentiality, Exclusivity, and Expenses, which shall be binding agreements of the parties.
