========================================================================
TERM SHEET
FOR SERIES SEED PREFERRED STOCK FINANCING OF
ZENITH HEALTH, INC.

Date: 9 September 2023

Issuer:           Zenith Health, Inc. (the "Company")
Investors:        Tiger Global Management (the "Lead Investor")
Series:           Series Seed Preferred Stock
Pre-Money Valuation:  $100.0 million
Amount of Financing:  $45.7 million
========================================================================

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms of the proposed financing.
Except for the binding provisions set forth below, this term sheet is
non-binding and is intended solely as a basis for further discussion.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Liquidation Preference
In the event of any liquidation, dissolution, or winding up of the Company, the holders of Series Series Seed Preferred Stock shall be entitled to receive, prior and in preference to any distribution to the holders of Common Stock, an amount per share equal to 3.0x the Original Purchase Price (the "Liquidation Preference"). No participation rights beyond the initial Liquidation Preference amount.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Price-Based Anti-Dilution
The Series Seed Preferred Stock will have full ratchet anti-dilution protection.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

BOARD COMPOSITION
The Company's Board shall be composed of 5 directors: 4 appointed by the Investors, 1 appointed by the Founders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Founder Vesting
All shares held by each Founder shall be subject to vesting over 2.0 years (24 months total), with a 12-month cliff. Shares shall vest monthly following the cliff on a pro-rata basis. No acceleration of vesting upon Change of Control.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

DIVIDEND RIGHTS
The Series Seed Preferred shall accrue cumulative dividends at 8% of the Original Issue Price, payable when and if declared by the Board.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Protective Provisions
So long as any shares of Series Seed Preferred Stock are outstanding, the Company shall not, without the prior written consent of the holders of at least a majority of the Series Seed Preferred Stock: (i) alter or change the rights of the Preferred Stock; (ii) increase or decrease the authorized number of shares; (iii) create any new class or series of shares having rights senior to or on parity with the Preferred Stock; (iv) declare or pay any dividend on Common Stock; (v) approve any merger, sale of substantially all assets, or other liquidation event; or (vi) increase the size of the Board of Directors.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Equity Incentive Pool
The Company's Option Pool shall be set at 20% of the fully-diluted post-money capitalization, to be created prior to the Closing.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Information Rights
The Company shall deliver to each Major Investor (i) annual audited financial statements within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year, (ii) unaudited quarterly financial statements within 45 days after the end of each quarter, and (iii) an annual budget and business plan within 30 days prior to the start of each fiscal year.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

ROFR AND CO-SALE AGREEMENT
Founders may not transfer shares without first offering them to the Company, then to the Investors. If the transfer proceeds, Investors have co-sale rights to participate on a pro-rata basis.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CONVERSION
The Series Seed Preferred converts to Common Stock at a 1:1 ratio (as adjusted). Automatic conversion occurs upon a Qualified IPO or majority Preferred consent.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Drag-Along Agreement
If holders of at least 50% of the outstanding shares approve a Sale of the Company, all other shareholders shall be required to vote in favor of such transaction and take all necessary actions to consummate it.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Redemption Rights
At any time after 2 years from the date of issuance, upon the request of the holders of at least a majority of the then-outstanding Series Seed Preferred Stock, the Company shall redeem all outstanding shares at the Original Purchase Price plus any declared but unpaid dividends.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

NO-SHOP AGREEMENT
For 120 days from execution of this term sheet, the Company and the Founders shall not directly or indirectly solicit, initiate, or encourage any competing financing proposal.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Expenses
The Company shall pay the reasonable legal fees and expenses of counsel to the Investors in connection with the financing, up to a maximum of $25,000.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Governing Law
This Term Sheet shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws provisions.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Conditions Precedent
The closing of the financing shall be subject to customary conditions, including: completion of due diligence satisfactory to the Investors; execution of definitive agreements; delivery of legal opinions; and all requisite corporate approvals.
