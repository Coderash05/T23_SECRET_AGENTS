========================================================================
TERM SHEET
FOR SERIES A PREFERRED STOCK FINANCING OF
PHOTON ROBOTICS, INC.

Date: 1 January 2024

Issuer:           Photon Robotics, Inc. (the "Company")
Investors:        Tiger Global Management (the "Lead Investor")
Series:           Series A Preferred Stock
Pre-Money Valuation:  $100.0 million
Amount of Financing:  $25.5 million
========================================================================

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms of the proposed financing.
Except for the binding provisions set forth below, this term sheet is
non-binding and is intended solely as a basis for further discussion.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

LIQUIDATION PREFERENCE
Upon any Liquidation Event, each holder of Series A Preferred shall receive a per-share amount equal to 3.0 times the Original Issue Price, plus any declared but unpaid dividends. After receiving the Liquidation Preference, the Series A Preferred shall participate pro rata with the Common Stock on an as-converted basis, with no cap.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

ANTI-DILUTION PROTECTION
In the event of a down-round issuance, the conversion price of the Series A Preferred shall be adjusted pursuant to a narrow-based weighted average formula.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

BOARD COMPOSITION
The Company's Board shall be composed of 5 directors: 2 appointed by the Investors, 2 appointed by the Founders, and 1 independent director(s) mutually agreed upon by the Common and Preferred holders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Founder Vesting
All shares held by each Founder shall be subject to vesting over 4.0 years (48 months total), with a 12-month cliff. Shares shall vest monthly following the cliff on a pro-rata basis. Vesting shall not accelerate under any circumstances.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Dividends
The holders of Series A Preferred Stock shall be entitled to receive non-cumulative dividends at a rate of 8% per annum on the Original Purchase Price. Dividends on Common Stock may not be paid unless all accrued Preferred dividends have been paid.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

PROTECTIVE PROVISIONS
The consent of the majority of Series A Preferred holders shall be required for: amendments to the Charter affecting the Preferred Stock; issuance of equity securities senior to the Series A Preferred; any Liquidation Event or Change of Control; changes to the Board size; and declaration of dividends on Common Stock.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Equity Incentive Pool
The Company's Option Pool shall be set at 15% of the fully-diluted post-money capitalization, to be created prior to the Closing.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Information Rights
The Company shall deliver to each Major Investor (i) annual audited financial statements within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year, (ii) unaudited quarterly financial statements within 45 days after the end of each quarter, and (iii) an annual budget and business plan within 30 days prior to the start of each fiscal year.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Right of First Refusal / Co-Sale
The Company shall have a right of first refusal on any proposed transfer of shares by a Founder. If the Company does not exercise its right, the Investors holding Series A Preferred Stock shall have a secondary right of first refusal, followed by a co-sale right allowing participation in the transfer pro rata.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Conversion Rights
Each share of Series A Preferred Stock shall be convertible, at the option of the holder, into shares of Common Stock at the then-applicable Conversion Rate (initially 1:1, subject to anti-dilution adjustments). All Series A Preferred Stock shall automatically convert into Common Stock upon (i) an IPO with aggregate proceeds of at least $50,000,000 or (ii) the consent of a majority of the Preferred holders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Drag-Along Agreement
If holders of at least 67% of the outstanding shares approve a Sale of the Company, all other shareholders shall be required to vote in favor of such transaction and take all necessary actions to consummate it.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Mandatory Redemption
The Series A Preferred Stock is redeemable at the option of the holders commencing 5 years from the Closing Date.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

NO-SHOP AGREEMENT
For 30 days from execution of this term sheet, the Company and the Founders shall not directly or indirectly solicit, initiate, or encourage any competing financing proposal.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Expenses
The Company shall pay the reasonable legal fees and expenses of counsel to the Investors in connection with the financing, up to a maximum of $25,000.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Conditions Precedent
The closing of the financing shall be subject to customary conditions, including: completion of due diligence satisfactory to the Investors; execution of definitive agreements; delivery of legal opinions; and all requisite corporate approvals.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Binding Provisions
This Term Sheet is non-binding, except for the provisions relating to Confidentiality, Exclusivity, and Expenses, which shall be binding agreements of the parties.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Governing Law
This Term Sheet shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws provisions.
