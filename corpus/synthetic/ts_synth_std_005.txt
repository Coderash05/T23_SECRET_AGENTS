========================================================================
TERM SHEET
FOR SERIES B PREFERRED STOCK FINANCING OF
QUANTUM LABS, INC.

Date: 6 January 2024

Issuer:           Quantum Labs, Inc. (the "Company")
Investors:        General Catalyst (the "Lead Investor")
Series:           Series B Preferred Stock
Pre-Money Valuation:  $30.0 million
Amount of Financing:  $9.1 million
========================================================================

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms of the proposed financing.
Except for the binding provisions set forth below, this term sheet is
non-binding and is intended solely as a basis for further discussion.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Liquidation Preference
In the event of any liquidation, dissolution, or winding up of the Company, the holders of Series Series B Preferred Stock shall be entitled to receive, prior and in preference to any distribution to the holders of Common Stock, an amount per share equal to 1.5x the Original Purchase Price (the "Liquidation Preference"). Holders of Series B Preferred shall not participate in remaining distributions beyond the Liquidation Preference.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Anti-Dilution Provisions
The Conversion Price of the Series B Preferred Stock shall be subject to broad-based weighted average anti-dilution adjustment in the event that the Company issues additional equity securities at a purchase price less than the then-effective Conversion Price.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

BOARD COMPOSITION
The Company's Board shall be composed of 4 directors: 1 appointed by the Investors, 2 appointed by the Founders, and 1 independent director(s) mutually agreed upon by the Common and Preferred holders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Founder Vesting
All shares held by each Founder shall be subject to vesting over 3.0 years (36 months total), with a 12-month cliff. Shares shall vest monthly following the cliff on a pro-rata basis. Vesting shall not accelerate under any circumstances.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Dividends
Dividends shall be cumulative at a rate of 8% per annum on the Series B Preferred Stock. No dividends shall be paid on Common Stock until all Preferred dividends are satisfied.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Protective Provisions
So long as any shares of Series B Preferred Stock are outstanding, the Company shall not, without the prior written consent of the holders of at least a majority of the Series B Preferred Stock: (i) alter or change the rights of the Preferred Stock; (ii) increase or decrease the authorized number of shares; (iii) create any new class or series of shares having rights senior to or on parity with the Preferred Stock; (iv) declare or pay any dividend on Common Stock; (v) approve any merger, sale of substantially all assets, or other liquidation event; or (vi) increase the size of the Board of Directors.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Option Pool
Prior to the closing of the financing, the Company shall reserve 10% of its post-money fully-diluted capitalization for issuance to directors, officers, employees, and consultants under the Company's equity incentive plan.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Information Rights
The Company shall deliver to each Major Investor (i) annual audited financial statements within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year, (ii) unaudited quarterly financial statements within 45 days after the end of each quarter, and (iii) an annual budget and business plan within 30 days prior to the start of each fiscal year.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

ROFR AND CO-SALE AGREEMENT
Founders may not transfer shares without first offering them to the Company, then to the Investors. If the transfer proceeds, Investors have co-sale rights to participate on a pro-rata basis.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Conversion Rights
Each share of Series B Preferred Stock shall be convertible, at the option of the holder, into shares of Common Stock at the then-applicable Conversion Rate (initially 1:1, subject to anti-dilution adjustments). All Series B Preferred Stock shall automatically convert into Common Stock upon (i) an IPO with aggregate proceeds of at least $50,000,000 or (ii) the consent of a majority of the Preferred holders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Drag-Along Agreement
If holders of at least 50% of the outstanding shares approve a Sale of the Company, all other shareholders shall be required to vote in favor of such transaction and take all necessary actions to consummate it.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Redemption Rights
At any time after 5 years from the date of issuance, upon the request of the holders of at least a majority of the then-outstanding Series B Preferred Stock, the Company shall redeem all outstanding shares at the Original Purchase Price plus any declared but unpaid dividends.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Exclusivity Period
The Founders agree to an exclusivity period of 30 days during which no competing offers shall be sought or entertained.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Confidentiality
The existence and terms of this Term Sheet shall be treated as confidential information by all parties and shall not be disclosed to any third party without the prior written consent of the other party, except as required by law.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Expenses
The Company shall pay the reasonable legal fees and expenses of counsel to the Investors in connection with the financing, up to a maximum of $25,000.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Binding Provisions
This Term Sheet is non-binding, except for the provisions relating to Confidentiality, Exclusivity, and Expenses, which shall be binding agreements of the parties.
