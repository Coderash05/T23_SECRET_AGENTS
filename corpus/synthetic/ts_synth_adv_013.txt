========================================================================
TERM SHEET
FOR SERIES A PREFERRED STOCK FINANCING OF
VANGUARD SOLUTIONS, INC.

Date: 16 October 2022

Issuer:           Vanguard Solutions, Inc. (the "Company")
Investors:        Bessemer Venture Partners (the "Lead Investor")
Series:           Series A Preferred Stock
Pre-Money Valuation:  $5.0 million
Amount of Financing:  $0.8 million
========================================================================

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms of the proposed financing.
Except for the binding provisions set forth below, this term sheet is
non-binding and is intended solely as a basis for further discussion.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

LIQUIDATION PREFERENCE
Upon any Liquidation Event, each holder of Series A Preferred shall receive a per-share amount equal to 3.0 times the Original Issue Price, plus any declared but unpaid dividends. Participation is capped at an aggregate of 5.0x the Original Purchase Price per share.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Price-Based Anti-Dilution
The Series A Preferred Stock will have full ratchet anti-dilution protection.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Board of Directors
The Board shall have 3 seats, allocated as follows: Investors shall designate 2 director(s), Founders shall designate 1 director(s).

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Founder Share Vesting
24 months total vesting schedule. Cliff: 12 months. Monthly vesting thereafter. Double-trigger acceleration: 100% of unvested shares shall accelerate upon a Change of Control followed by termination without Cause within 12 months.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Dividends
Dividends shall be cumulative at a rate of 8% per annum on the Series A Preferred Stock. No dividends shall be paid on Common Stock until all Preferred dividends are satisfied.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

PROTECTIVE PROVISIONS
The consent of the majority of Series A Preferred holders shall be required for: amendments to the Charter affecting the Preferred Stock; issuance of equity securities senior to the Series A Preferred; any Liquidation Event or Change of Control; changes to the Board size; and declaration of dividends on Common Stock.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

EMPLOYEE OPTION POOL
An option pool equal to 30% of the post-money fully-diluted shares shall be established prior to closing.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

INFORMATION AND INSPECTION RIGHTS
Major Investors shall receive annual audited financials, quarterly unaudited financials, monthly management reports, and an annual operating budget.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Right of First Refusal / Co-Sale
The Company shall have a right of first refusal on any proposed transfer of shares by a Founder. If the Company does not exercise its right, the Investors holding Series A Preferred Stock shall have a secondary right of first refusal, followed by a co-sale right allowing participation in the transfer pro rata.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Conversion Rights
Each share of Series A Preferred Stock shall be convertible, at the option of the holder, into shares of Common Stock at the then-applicable Conversion Rate (initially 1:1, subject to anti-dilution adjustments). All Series A Preferred Stock shall automatically convert into Common Stock upon (i) an IPO with aggregate proceeds of at least $50,000,000 or (ii) the consent of a majority of the Preferred holders.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Drag-Along
Shareholders holding at least 25% of the shares may compel all remaining shareholders to participate in an approved Sale of the Company.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Section – Mandatory Redemption
The Series A Preferred Stock is redeemable at the option of the holders commencing 2 years from the Closing Date.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Exclusivity / No-Shop
The Company agrees that for a period of 120 days following the execution of this Term Sheet, it shall not solicit, encourage, or accept any proposal from any other party for the financing of the Company.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Conditions Precedent
The closing of the financing shall be subject to customary conditions, including: completion of due diligence satisfactory to the Investors; execution of definitive agreements; delivery of legal opinions; and all requisite corporate approvals.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Expenses
The Company shall pay the reasonable legal fees and expenses of counsel to the Investors in connection with the financing, up to a maximum of $25,000.
